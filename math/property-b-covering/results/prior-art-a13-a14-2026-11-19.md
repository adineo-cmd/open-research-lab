## What is established prior art (do not claim as new)

1. **m(3) = 7 and the Fano plane.** Wikipedia's *Property B* article
   (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Property_B, retrieved 2026-11-19) records
   m(n) = 1,3,7,23 for n = 1..4 and identifies the Steiner triple system S7
   (the Fano plane) as the smallest 3-uniform collection without Property B.
   That the Fano plane is the *unique* 7-edge non-2-colourable 3-graph is a
   classical fact in this area (Toft, *On colour-critical hypergraphs*, 1975,
   is the natural reference). We re-certified it computationally here
   (`fano_uniqueness_n{7..10}_e7.json` all INFEASIBLE; ⌊21/2⌋ = 10 makes
   n = 7..10 exhaustive), but the fact itself is not a discovery.
2. **The degree-bound lemma** (edge-minimal non-2-colourable hypergraph has
   minimum degree ≥ 2) is elementary and standard.
3. It is plausible that the *classification of small critical 3-graphs*
   already includes the "no Fano-free 8-edge example" statement (again Toft
   1975 and follow-ups). With websearch unavailable and the accessible
   arxiv/OEIS searches returning nothing, this could not be confirmed or
   refuted today. **No uniqueness claim is made for the probe's theorem.**
