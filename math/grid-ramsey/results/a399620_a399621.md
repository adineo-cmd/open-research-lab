## Negative results (recorded per lab principle 3)

1. **Quad model (v1)** — one mono bool per rectangle via 4 reified star
   equalities: proves 5×5/6×6 but **stalls on proofs from 7×7 up**
   (300 s → objective 21, bound 8). Kept as `--model quad`.
2. **Count model (v2) without the analytic LB**: 7×7 finds 21 fast but
   bound stalls at 13 after 180 s. The LB constraint is what makes
   certification tractable.
3. **Simulated annealing** (`tools/sa_search.py`, 12 procs × 100 s,
   17M steps/proc): best verified 104 for 9×9 — far worse than CP-SAT's
   80. Exact delta computation per flip was used; the annealing schedule
   was simply not competitive on this landscape. SA abandoned.
4. 9×9 closing the last gap 74→80: **not achieved** in this session
   (900 s CP-SAT + 100 s SA). The composition completes the moment any
   construction at 74 is found; rerun is one command (see README).

## Reproduce

```bash
pip install --user --break-system-packages ortools   # 9.15 used
python3 tools/relax_lb.py 9 9                        # → lb: 74
python3 tools/min_mono_rect.py 3 9 --lb 3            # → OPTIMAL 3.0
python3 tools/min_mono_rect.py 9 9 --lb 74 --time-limit 900
python3 tools/sa_search.py 9 9 --time 100            # heuristic UB
```
